## Band Exhaustion Analysis

### Concept
Market moves in waves; exhaustion indicates reversal potential.

### Measurement
```
Exhaustion Score = (Current Move / ATR) * Volume Ratio * Time Factor
Where:
- Current Move: Distance from swing point
- ATR: 14-period Average True Range
- Volume Ratio: Current volume / 20-period average
- Time Factor: Decay based on move duration
```

### Thresholds
| Score | Interpretation | Action |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| < 0.5 | Early stage | Look for continuation |
| 0.5-1.0 | Normal | Monitor |
| 1.0-1.5 | Extended | Reduce position size |
| > 1.5 | Exhausted | Prepare for reversal |

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

### Confluence Scoring
- Single timeframe: Base signal
- 2 timeframe confluence: +25% confidence
- 3 timeframe confluence: +50% confidence
- 4+ timeframe confluence: +75% confidence

### Divergence Detection
- Price making higher highs, indicator lower = Bearish divergence
- Price making lower lows, indicator higher = Bullish divergence
- Multi-timeframe divergence = Stronger signal
